# Roadmap / ideas

This is a backlog, **not a release schedule**.

## Next technical foundation

- add safe persistence using stable city identifiers;
- verify save/load and world switching before growing the simulation;
- reduce reflection-heavy discovery where stable game/NML APIs exist;
- keep the current working Economy UI architecture unless a replacement passes regression testing.

## Economy expansion

- improve reserve/need models;
- production and consumption pressure;
- markets / market buildings;
- additional prosperity factors;
- clearer history and trend visualization.

## Trade expansion

- improve internal trade routing;
- neighboring-state trade;
- international trade;
- visible caravans / trade actors;
- trade disruptions and route conditions.

## Larger systems

- monopolies;
- economic systems / policy models;
- government offices / tax collectors;
- optional compatibility addons.

## Political World bridge

Only after international trade is stable: create a separate addon that lets Political World politics/diplomacy interact with TradeBox economics without making either core mod depend on the other.
